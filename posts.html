My University Major

I study Computer Science at university. Computer Science is a major that focuses on computers, programming, and technology. In this major, students learn how to write programs, create websites, and solve different problems using computers. We also study topics like databases, computer networks, and software development. This major helps students understand how computers work and how to use technology in different areas. After graduation, Computer Science students can work in many fields. They can become software developers, web developers, database administrators, or work in cybersecurity. Some graduates also work in artificial intelligence and data analysis. Computer Science is an important major because technology is used in almost every part of our daily lives. It gives students different skills and many career opportunities in the future.
